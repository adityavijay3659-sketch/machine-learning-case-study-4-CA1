BERT already learned English from millions of sentences, so with only 1440 training reviews
and 3 epochs it beats an LSTM that has to learn word meanings from scratch (transfer learning).
Neutral is the hardest class for both models because 3-star reviews mix praise and complaints.
Deployment: bert-base is ~110M params and slow on CPU, so in production use a small/distilled BERT,
quantize it, cap max_length, batch requests, or use GPU. LSTM is tiny and fast but less accurate.
Also watch for data drift (new slang, new products) and retrain periodically.
